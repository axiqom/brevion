# Brevion Systems — Brand Guidelines v1.0

Source: Mendel WhatsApp drop 2026-07-27. Treat as source of truth for visual identity.

---

# Brand Philosophy

**Engineering, simplified.**

Brevion Systems builds products where precision, consistency, and trust are non-negotiable. Every element of the brand should reflect the same principles found in world-class engineering: clean, intentional, and built to last.

The brand should never feel loud or flashy. Confidence comes from restraint.

---

# Brand Attributes

* Modern
* Engineered
* Premium
* Technical
* Human
* Confident
* Precise
* Minimal

Avoid appearing:

* Industrial cliché
* Heavy construction
* Cheap
* Overly futuristic
* Corporate blue
* Busy

# Logo

## Primary Logo

The primary logo consists of:

* Brevion Mark
* BREVION wordmark
* SYSTEMS descriptor

Always use the complete lockup unless space requires otherwise.

## Icon

The icon represents precision, structure, manufacturing, engineered geometry, machined forms.

It should never be explained as a "B." The discovery is part of the identity.

## Clear Space

Minimum clear space: Height of the center square inside the icon. Nothing should enter this space.

## Minimum Size

- Print: 0.75"
- Digital: 48px
- Icon only: 24px minimum

# Color System

## Primary Brand Color — Brevion Gold

- HEX: `#FFB668`
- RGB: 255 182 104
- Purpose: Primary CTA accents, logo accents, highlights, icons, interactive states, links, progress indicators
- Hero color — never exceed roughly **10–15%** of any layout

## Secondary Accent — Brevion Light Gold

- HEX: `#FDCE7E`
- Purpose: Hover states, illustrations, large soft backgrounds, gradients, data visualization, supporting accents
- Never replace the primary gold

## Primary Neutral — Carbon Taupe

- HEX: `#453F3A`
- Purpose: Logo, headlines, navigation, body text, footer, icons
- Replaces pure black

## Secondary Neutral — Warm Aluminum

- HEX: `#B8AEA1`
- Purpose: Borders, dividers, secondary text, UI components, disabled states, diagrams

## Background — Porcelain

- HEX: `#F5F2ED`
- Purpose: Primary background, cards, white space, forms
- Avoid pure white unless absolutely necessary

## Color Ratios

Typical page:

- **65%** Porcelain
- **20%** Carbon Taupe
- **10%** Warm Aluminum
- **5%** Brevion Gold

# Typography

## Primary Typeface — Sora

Used throughout the entire identity.

### Heading Weights

Bold, SemiBold

### Body

Regular, Medium

### Tracking

- BREVION — default
- SYSTEMS — increase tracking by roughly 200–300 for a technical, engineered feel

Note: An earlier Mendel voice request used Manrope for body. **This guideline supersedes that** — Sora for headings and body.

---

# Photography

Emphasize: CNC machining, toolpaths, machined aluminum, clean workspaces, robots, aerospace components, inspection equipment, surface finishes, engineering teams, minimal compositions.

Avoid: sparks, grunge, dirty factories, workers posing, generic stock imagery.

---

# Iconography

Outline or solid, geometric, 2px stroke, rounded internal corners, minimal, grid-based.

---

# UI Principles

Large white space. Large typography. Minimal decoration. Every element intentional. If something doesn't improve clarity, remove it.

---

# Buttons

- **Primary:** Carbon Taupe background, Brevion Gold hover
- **Secondary:** Outline, Carbon border, Porcelain background
- **Text links:** Carbon, hover Brevion Gold

---

# Shapes

Prefer squares, rounded rectangles, 90°, 45°, consistent corner radius. Avoid circles unless functionally necessary. Everything should feel machined.

---

# Motion

Fast. Purposeful. No bouncing. No exaggerated easing. Resemble machinery: controlled, smooth, predictable.

---

# Voice & Tone

Write like an engineer. Not a salesperson.

Instead of "We strive to deliver exceptional manufacturing solutions..." → "Precision isn't optional."
Instead of "High quality products." → "Built to tolerance."

---

# Brand Keywords

Precision, Reliability, Repeatability, Systems, Engineering, Confidence, Manufacturing, Performance, Simplicity

---

# Design Inspiration

Visual language sits between Apple Hardware, Anduril, Linear, Porsche Design, Leica, Braun — clarity, restraint, precision.

## Brand Rule

**If you're unsure whether to add something, don't.**
